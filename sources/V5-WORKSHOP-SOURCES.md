## Current boundaries

Personal Google sign-in, participant configuration/import/review, Exa search, owned Netlify publication and the advertising wrapper are workshop additions. Source v5 already supplies guarded transport, research, history and channel readback.

Automatic reply sending, meeting scheduling writes, attendance association and physical postal fulfillment are not wired. Automatic whole-social-campaign activation and ad launch/restart are disabled in the portable package. The guide states the exact preparatory, read-only or conditional result for each channel.

The local Codex routine prepares work. The terminal dispatches exact approved actions and reads provider outcomes. A timer alone cannot create the next play. The computer must remain awake and online.
